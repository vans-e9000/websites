What we do

Building and civil engineering services for your project.

Every project has its own requirements. Twavwane brings together building capability, civil engineering thinking and practical coordination to help clients plan well and deliver with confidence.

The Twavwane approach

From foundations to final details

Our services are structured to make the process easier to understand. We can support a defined scope of work or help coordinate several connected parts of a project, with a focus on quality, safety and useful communication.
