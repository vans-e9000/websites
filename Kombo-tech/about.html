About Kombo Technical

We are a locally registered construction company committed to excellence, quality, and sustainable development across Tanzania.

Our Story

Kombo Technical Services Group Limited was established with a vision to transform Tanzania's construction landscape. Founded by Robert Soka, the company was first registered with the Contractors Registration Board (CRB) as a Building Contractor in 1997. It added its Civil Contractor registration in 2002. These are the two classes it continues to hold.

Over the years we have delivered projects across the residential, commercial and infrastructure sectors, building a reputation for quality workmanship, safe sites and clients who come back to us. Our registrations authorize us to tender for and execute both private and public sector contracts nationwide.

We pride ourselves on being a truly Tanzanian company, deeply rooted in local expertise, materials and supply chains, while embracing international construction standards and best practices.

Robert Soka

Managing Director

Robert Soka is the Managing Director of Kombo Technical Services Group. An experienced civil and building construction professional, he founded the company and grew it into a CRB-registered contractor serving clients across Tanzania.

He provides strategic direction and stays involved in day-to-day delivery. He reviews programmes, plans site resources and checks work quality before project handover.
