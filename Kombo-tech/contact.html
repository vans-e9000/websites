What Happens Next

1. We Review Your Enquiry

Our team reads your message and gets in touch to clarify scope, site location, timeline and budget range.

2. Site Visit & Assessment

We inspect the site, take measurements and review any drawings you already have, so your price is based on real conditions rather than assumptions.

3. Detailed Quotation

You receive a priced bill of quantities, a proposed programme of works and a clear explanation of the scope and exclusions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get a quote?

Fill out the contact form above or call us directly. We'll schedule a site visit to assess your project and provide a detailed, no-obligation quote.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Dar es Salaam but serve clients across Tanzania. Our CRB registration allows us to undertake projects nationwide.

What is your typical project timeline?

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. During our initial consultation, we'll provide a detailed timeline specific to your project.

Are you registered with CRB?

Yes, we hold two CRB registrations: Class 6 Civil Contractor (C6/0326/12/2002) and Class 7 Building Contractor (B7/0330/7/1997).

Do you handle both design and construction?

Yes. We work with architects and engineers to develop buildable, cost-effective designs, or we can build from drawings you already have.

What do you need to price my project?

Drawings or a clear description of the works, the site location, and your target timeline. With those, we can schedule a site visit and prepare a detailed quotation.
