Quality Assurance 05 / Compliance

Laboratory verified quality control

Every structural element is verified against engineering tolerances. We conduct site slump tests on wet concrete, crush-test concrete cubes at 7-day and 28-day curing intervals, and measure sub-base soil compaction with certified gauges.

Test certificates are catalogued systematically and submitted with monthly interim valuation claims, providing clients and supervising consultant engineers with verifiable proof of specification compliance.

Client Queries 06 / Information

Frequently addressed operational questions

Practical details on contract administration, site possession, mobilization timelines, and tender pricing standards.

What documents are required to prepare a formal quote?

We require architectural plans, structural engineering drawings, bills of quantities (if available), and verified site coordinates in Dar es Salaam. Where a full bill of quantities is not yet drafted, our quantity surveyors calculate quantities directly from approved drawings.

How rapidly can ASABHI mobilize to project sites in Dar es Salaam?

Plant and site setup teams mobilize promptly following contract signing and site handover. We establish secure site camps, water points, and power connections in parallel with ground clearing.

How are design variations and site instructions managed?

All scope changes require a written Site Instruction (SI) signed by the client or supervising consultant. We cost the variation against agreed contract rates and receive client authorization before physical execution begins.

Does ASABHI provide performance guarantees and advance payment bonds?

Yes. As a registered Class 1 contractor, we arrange commercial bank guarantees, performance bonds, and advance payment securities in accordance with statutory and standard contract provisions.

Have a project ready for contractor mobilization?

Submit your engineering drawings and bill of quantities. Our technical estimation department in Dar es Salaam provides itemized, transparent quotations.
