A licensed Tanzanian contractor structured for complete construction delivery.

ASABHI COMPANY LIMITED is incorporated under the laws of Tanzania and registered with the Contractors Registration Board (CRB). Headquartered at ADA Estate in Kinondoni, Dar es Salaam, our engineering teams deploy directly to commercial and public works across the city.

Corporate Identity 01 / Foundation

Tanzanian engineering accountability

ASABHI COMPANY LIMITED was established to provide public authorities, commercial developers, and institutional clients with dependable construction contracting in Dar es Salaam. We maintain full registration in Building Works (Class 1), Civil Engineering (Class 1), and Electrical Installations (Class 3).

Because all three licenses are held under our direct corporate umbrella, our project managers resolve structural and electrical coordination in-house. Subcontracting handoffs that typically cause delays on commercial job sites are eliminated through unified site oversight.

Health & Safety 03 / HSE Policy

Occupational health, safety, and environmental standards

Every active ASABHI project site operates under a site-specific Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) plan. We enforce 100% compliance with personal protective equipment (PPE), daily morning toolbox safety briefings, perimeter fencing, and clear emergency response protocols.

  • Mandatory PPE compliance for all employees, trade crews, and visitors
  • Daily morning hazard identification talks prior to machine operation
  • Certified scaffolding, ladder inspections, and fall-arrest harnesses
  • Environmentally responsible waste disposal and construction dust control
Zero-Harm Safety Target Across All Active Project Sites
