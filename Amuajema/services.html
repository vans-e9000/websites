What we deliver

Construction services with the site in mind.

Our work covers the connected parts of a project, from setting out and structure through finishes, external works and improvements to existing buildings.

01 · New builds

Building construction

End-to-end construction support for residential, commercial and institutional buildings, with the work planned around the structure, use and site conditions.

  • Site preparation and setting out
  • Foundations, walls and structural works
  • Roofing, plastering and finishes
  • Coordination of building trades

02 · Site works

Civil works and external works

Supporting infrastructure that helps a property work properly through different seasons and everyday use.

  • Drainage and stormwater solutions
  • Access ways, paving and yards
  • Ground preparation and levelling
  • Boundary and supporting structures

03 · Improvements

Renovation and fit-out

Measured improvements for buildings that need a better layout, refreshed finishes or a new purpose.

  • Space reconfiguration
  • Floor, wall and ceiling finishes
  • Doors, windows and joinery
  • Commercial and office fit-outs

04 · Support

Maintenance and project support

Planned and responsive work that helps owners protect the value and function of their buildings after handover.

  • Building condition checks
  • Repairs and corrective works
  • Planned maintenance schedules
  • Small works and extensions

Common questions

Answers before the first site visit.

Clear answers help owners, developers and project teams decide what information to prepare.

What construction work does Amuajema Contractors handle?

Amuajema Contractors handles building construction, specialist civil works, renovation and fit-out, external works, maintenance and project support for residential, commercial and institutional properties.

Where does Amuajema Contractors work?

Amuajema Contractors is a local Tanzanian contractor based in Mwanza and can consider projects across Tanzania according to the scope, programme, access and site requirements.

What should I include in a project enquiry?

Include the project region, building type, current stage, approximate size, required work, expected timing and any drawings, bills of quantities or site photos available.
