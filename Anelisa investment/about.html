How we work

Three rules that do not change with the job size.

They are simple to write down and harder to keep. Keeping them is the point.

01

Scope before price

A number without a scope is a guess. We write down what is included, what is excluded, and what would change the price.

02

Records on site

Deliveries, instructions, and stage checks are recorded while the work is happening, not reconstructed at the end.

03

Local access

The office is in Nzega. When something needs to be seen, it can be seen the same week.

On site

The same team from the first visit to the handover file.

Work is followed by the people who priced it. That keeps the scope, the programme, and the site records in one place instead of passing between departments.

  • One point of contact for the client and the site team.
  • A written scope before work starts.
  • Records kept on site and handed over at the end.
Next step

Come to the office, or send the site details first.

Both work. A visit is useful when the ground, the access, or an existing building is part of the question.
