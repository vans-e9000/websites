What happens next

  • We read the brief and check the scope against the site.

  • You receive a written quote with inclusions, exclusions, and the programme.

For tenders, include the submission deadline and the document reference.

How a price is built

The quote follows the scope, not the other way round.

Where the drawings are complete, the price is built from measured quantities. Where they are not, the uncertain parts are listed separately and measured as the work proceeds.

Anything outside the scope is written down as an exclusion. That keeps the first number and the final account close together.

  • Inclusions listed by stage and trade.
  • Exclusions named before work starts.
  • Changes priced in writing before they are built.
