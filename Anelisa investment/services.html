Method

The method is the same on every package.

The size of the job changes the programme. It does not change the order of the checks.

  • Set out against the drawings and the levels on site.
  • Agree access and storage with everyone who works there.
  • Inspect each stage before it is covered by the next.
  • Hand over with the records that show what was built.
Materials

Checked on delivery, not after they are built in.

Cement, reinforcement, blocks, and timber are checked against the specification when they arrive. Anything that does not match is held back before it reaches the work.

  • Delivery checks against the specification
  • Storage that protects the material
  • A record for each batch used
