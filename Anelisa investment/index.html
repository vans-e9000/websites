How a project runs

Three steps from enquiry to handover.

No stage starts until the one before it has been checked. That applies to the paperwork as much as the concrete.

01

Site visit and set-out

We look at the ground, the access, and any existing structures. The scope is checked against what the site can support before a price is fixed.

02

Sequence and materials

The work is broken into stages, with the materials and trades each stage needs. Dates are set against the site, not against a wish.

03

Build, check, hand over

Each stage is inspected before the next covers it. Handover includes the drawings, approvals, and maintenance notes we hold.
