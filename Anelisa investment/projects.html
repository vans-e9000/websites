Verification

We do not publish work we cannot evidence.

A project claim needs the scope, the location, the dates, the client's permission, and photographs of the finished work. Where any of those is missing, the project stays off the site.

Before you send a brief

Three things that make a first price possible.

You do not need a finished drawing set. You do need enough detail for the site and the scope to be understood.

01

The site

Where it is, how it is reached, what the levels do, and what already stands there. A phone photo of the plot is useful.

02

The scope

Drawings, room counts, areas, or a written description. Say what is included and what someone else will handle.

03

The timing

When you want to start, when it has to finish, and which stage cannot move. That sets the order of the work.

Next step

Send the brief. We will tell you what is missing.
