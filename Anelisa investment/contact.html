Before you call

Three details that make the first call useful.

You do not need a finished drawing set. You do need enough for the site and the scope to be understood.

01

The location

The ward, street, or a pin. Say whether the plot is inside Nzega or further out, and how a vehicle reaches it.

02

The work type

Building, civil, or coordination. If it is a mix, say which part is already covered by someone else.

03

The timing

When you want to start and when it has to finish. A fixed deadline changes the sequence, not just the price.
