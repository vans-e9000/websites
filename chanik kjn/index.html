Karibu

A Botanical Love Story in the Heart of Tanzania

Nestled in Chanika, Dar es Salaam, our gardens are a living canvas of jacaranda avenues, towering baobabs, and rare orchids — framed by the warm breeze of the Indian Ocean. From an intimate kifungo under the canopy to a grand harusi beneath the stars, every celebration is woven with Tanzanian soul.

Our team blends modern luxury with Swahili tradition — kanga-draped courtyards, madafu bars, and Tingatinga-inspired florals — so your day feels unmistakably, beautifully Tanzania. Explore the Gardens arrow_forward
