Bustani

A Living Canvas of the Swahili Coast

Since 2009 we have nurtured this green sanctuary in Chanika, Dar es Salaam — home to over 200 plant species. Walk beneath a purple jacaranda canopy in October, meet a baobab that has stood for three centuries, and lose yourself in a glasshouse of 3,000 orchids.

Every path is lined with stories — Tingatinga-inspired flowerbeds, kanga-bright tropical borders, and quiet corners where the city fades into birdsong.
