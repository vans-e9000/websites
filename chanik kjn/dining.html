Milo

From Our Gardens to Your Plate

Our chefs grow herbs, fruits and spices right in the gardens. Coconut milk is pressed fresh each morning, and firewood for the mishkaki is lit at noon. This is food the way Tanzania tastes — honest, generous, and full of flavor.

Whether it's a sundowner over the lawns, a business lunch, or a family dinner after a day at the park, there's a table waiting for you — with a madafu in hand.
