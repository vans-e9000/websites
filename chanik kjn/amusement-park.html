Burudani

Six Acres of Pure Furaha

From the heart-stopping Wind Rider coaster to a lazy splash in the lagoon, our park is designed for every age — toddlers in the Kids' Cove, adrenaline seekers on the coasters, and sunset romantics on the Star Wheel.

When night falls, the park glows — fairy lights, food stalls serving chipsi mayai and mishkaki, and the skyline of Dar twinkling from the top of the wheel.
