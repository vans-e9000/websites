About Androsan / 03

A construction partner for work that has to function every day.

Androsan is a Tanzania-focused contractor for civil works, building works and project coordination. The company works from the actual conditions of each site rather than a fixed delivery script.

The working approach

Good delivery depends on decisions being made at the right time.

At the start, Androsan works to understand the location, intended use, drawings, access, materials and programme. During delivery, the focus shifts to sequence, workmanship, records and the decisions that can affect time or cost.

The client remains responsible for approvals, funding decisions and the information they control. Androsan's role is to keep the work package clear and make site progress visible to the people responsible for the project.

A clear working relationship

What does the client provide?

The client shares the project objective, available drawings and site information, required approvals, decision contacts and known operating constraints.

What is clarified before work?

The parties review scope, responsibilities, assumptions, interfaces and timing, then identify information still needed for the next decision.

Why keep site records?

Current notes on progress, decisions and changes help the people responsible for a project stay aligned with the agreed work.

Androsan in practice

Clear information gives the site team a better chance to do the work once.
