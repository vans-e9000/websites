A useful first enquiry
01 / Where

Locate the site

Include the region or district, access route and whether the site is active or occupied.

02 / What

Describe the work

Tell us what needs to be built, repaired, extended or coordinated and its intended use.

03 / When

Share the timing

Note target dates, operating limits and who will review or approve the next step.

Project enquiry

Use the form for civil works, building works or project coordination in Tanzania. Short answers are fine. A location and a clear description of the work are the most useful starting points.

Before you send

Do I need complete drawings?

No. Send what is available and explain what is still being prepared. The missing information can be identified during the review.

Can Androsan review an existing site?

Include the site location, current condition, access constraints and the work you want assessed in the project information field.

What happens after the form?

This local form confirms the enquiry on screen. Connect it to Androsan's approved email or enquiry system before public launch.
