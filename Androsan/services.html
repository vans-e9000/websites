Capabilities / 01

Defined work packages for the parts of construction that matter.

Androsan aligns the work package with the site, drawings, available materials, access conditions and the client decisions that control delivery.

01

Civil works

Civil work changes how a site is reached, drained and used. Androsan plans the work around levels, ground conditions, water flow, vehicle movement and maintenance access.

Typical scope

  • Site clearance and earthworks
  • Formation and subgrade preparation
  • Access roads and hardstand areas
  • Drainage channels and culverts
  • External site works

Information to confirm

Site levels, soil information, drainage routes, available construction materials, access for plant and the intended use of the finished area.

02

Building works

Building delivery depends on a controlled sequence. Groundworks, structure, enclosure, services and finishes need to be coordinated so one trade does not create rework for another.

Typical scope

  • Setting out and foundations
  • Concrete and masonry work
  • Roofing and weather protection
  • Internal and external finishes
  • External works around the building

Information to confirm

Approved drawings, specifications, structural requirements, material standards, access restrictions, utility connections and the required completion date.

03

Project coordination

Coordination gives the client and site team a shared record of what has been agreed, what is changing and what must happen next.

Working outputs

  • Scope and responsibility review
  • Work sequence and programme updates
  • Site progress records
  • Open decisions and change tracking
  • Completion and handover information

Information to confirm

Decision makers, reporting frequency, approval route, consultant responsibilities, procurement method and any operational constraints around the site.

Before a work package is defined

What affects the scope?

The work package depends on the project brief and verified site information. This overview is a starting point, not a construction specification.

  • DrawingsCurrent plans, specifications and revisions available for review.
  • Site factsLevels, ground information, utilities, drainage and access limits.
  • InterfacesOther teams, consultants or site operations that may affect the work.
  • ProgrammeTarget dates, work windows and dependencies that need coordination.
