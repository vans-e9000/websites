Questions

Before you get in touch

What clients ask before their first contact.

What should I send with the enquiry?

Send drawings or a written scope, the site location and the date you need the work complete. If drawings are not ready, describe the building and the office arranges a site visit to measure the work.

How long does a quotation take?

The quotation is issued after the site visit and measurement. The timeline is confirmed at the visit, once the scope and access are understood.

Can I request a site visit without drawings?

Yes. Describe the building, the location and the handover date. A visit is arranged and the scope is measured on site.
