About

A Tanzanian contractor built around three registered trades.

BMX Contractors Limited is registered with the Contractors Registration Board for building, civil and electrical work, and runs all three from a single office in Kinondoni, Dar es Salaam.

Managing Director: Edward Olomi Head office: Kinondoni, Dar es Salaam

The company

One registration, three trades, one site team

BMX Contractors Limited was formed to carry building, civil and electrical packages under one appointment, so a client does not have to coordinate three separate contractors on the same plot.

The building, civil and electrical certificates are held by the same company and the same management. Site supervision, procurement and reporting therefore follow one method across all three disciplines, and a variation in one trade is priced against the programme for the whole project.

  • CRB building, civil and electrical registrations
  • One appointment covers structure, groundworks and power
  • Site supervision by registered contractors
  • Written weekly progress and variation records

Leadership

The people you deal with

Enquiries, pricing and site decisions pass through the Managing Director and the project team, which keeps the gap between a drawing revision and a decision on site short.

    Mobilisation

    Working beyond the city

    Site teams mobilise from the Kinondoni office, and the quotation states travel, supervision and material logistics before award.

    • Estimating

      Drawings, scope and site conditions priced from the office before a quotation is issued.

    • Travel

      Transport and accommodation for the site team costed in the quotation rather than added later.

    • Site set-up

      Storage, power and welfare agreed with the client before the first trade starts.

    • Handover

      As-built drawings, commissioning sheets and a defects schedule issued from the office at completion.

    Working method

    What every engagement carries

    The same four commitments apply to every scope, whether the work is a new building or a single installation.

    • Registered supervision on every active working day
    • Weekly written progress, materials and variation records
    • Method statements and site safety set before trades start
    • As-built drawings, commissioning sheets and a defects schedule at handover
    • Variations priced and approved in writing before the extra work proceeds
