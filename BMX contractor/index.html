The company

Registered to carry the work, staffed to finish it.

BMX Contractors Limited runs building, civil and electrical teams from one office in Kinondoni, Dar es Salaam. The three CRB registrations sit with the same company, so a project that spans structure, groundworks and power stays with one contractor instead of three separate appointments.

Edward Olomi leads the company as Managing Director. Enquiries, pricing and site decisions pass through him and the project team, which keeps the gap between a drawing revision and a decision on site short.

Method

How a BMX contract runs

The sequence is fixed before mobilisation. Each stage ends with a document the client keeps.

01

Scope and site visit

We walk the site, check what the drawings assume and measure the quantities the price is built on.

02

Programme and procurement

Materials, plant and labour are scheduled against the handover date, with long-lead items listed first.

03

Construction

Supervisors run the trades on site and report weekly on progress, variations and the next inspection.

04

Handover

Commissioning records, as-built drawings and a defects schedule are issued with the building.

Questions

Before you send drawings

Four answers that decide how an enquiry is handled.

Which registrations does BMX hold?

BMX Contractors Limited holds CRB Building B2/330/07/2026 at Class 2, CRB Civil C3/250/07/2026 at Class 3 and CRB Electrical E2/174/11/2026 at Class 2.

Can one contract cover building, civil and electrical work?

Yes. The three registrations belong to the same company, so a single appointment can include groundworks, structure, finishes and the electrical installation. Programme and site supervision are then coordinated by one team.

Where is the office?

The office is in Kinondoni, Dar es Salaam, with post addressed to P.O. Box 13462. Site visits are arranged by phone or email before a quotation is issued.

What do you need to price a job?

Send the drawings or a written scope, the site location and the date you need the work complete. If drawings are not ready, describe the building and we will arrange a site visit to measure the work.
