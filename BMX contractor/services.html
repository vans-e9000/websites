Services

Building, civil and electrical services from one registered contractor.

Each discipline is registered with the Contractors Registration Board and delivered by the same company. Scope, programme and site supervision are agreed once, then applied across all three trades.

Building works · Class 2 Civil works · Class 3 Electrical works · Class 2

01 / Building works

Structure and envelope, finished to handover

Building work starts with setting out and foundations, moves through the reinforced concrete frame, and ends with roofing, waterproofing, partitions and finishes. Class 2 registration covers commercial, residential and institutional buildings.

A building package can be awarded whole, or as a structure-only scope when the client has a separate finishing team. Either way, the concrete, blockwork and roof are signed off by the same supervisors.

  • Excavation, setting out and foundations
  • Reinforced concrete columns, beams and slabs
  • Blockwork, partitions and plaster
  • Roofing, waterproofing and rainwater goods
  • Doors, windows, joinery and finishes
  • External paving, boundary walls and gates
Price building works

02 / Civil works

Ground, access and drainage around the building

Civil work covers what sits under and around a structure: ground preparation, vehicle access, surface water and ground retention. Class 3 registration covers roads, drainage and external works packages.

Teams work around live buildings with traffic, dust and access controls agreed before mobilisation, so tenants and neighbours keep a usable route through the site.

  • Site clearance and bulk earthworks
  • Sub-base, base course and compaction
  • Concrete paving, kerbs and vehicle crossings
  • Storm drainage, culverts and soakaways
  • Retaining walls and slope protection
  • Service trenches and reinstatement
Price civil works

03 / Electrical works

From main intake to the last fitting

Electrical installation is delivered by our Class 2 registered team: main intake, distribution boards, submains, containment and final circuits. Work follows the drawings and is coordinated with the building trades so conduits and boxes are set before plaster.

At completion the client receives a circuit schedule, insulation readings and as-built drawings, which makes future maintenance and any fit-out change straightforward.

  • Main distribution boards and submains
  • Cable tray, trunking and conduit containment
  • Lighting, socket and fixed equipment circuits
  • Earthing, bonding and surge protection
  • Generator and changeover connections
  • Commissioning, schedules and as-built records

In every package

What the contract carries

Four commitments apply to every scope, whether the work is a new building, a drainage run or a distribution board replacement.

01

Registered supervision

A CRB-registered contractor signs the works, with a named supervisor on site for every active day of the programme.

02

Weekly reporting

Progress, materials on site, variations and the next inspection are reported in writing each week.

03

Site safety

Method statements, protective equipment and edge protection are set before trades start work on site.

04

Handover records

As-built drawings, commissioning sheets and a defects schedule are issued at practical completion.

Questions

Scope and appointments

What clients ask before awarding a package.

Do you work as a subcontractor?

Yes. Structural, civil and electrical packages can be taken under a main contractor, with your programme and reporting format adopted for the duration of the works.

Can work start before the drawings are complete?

Groundworks and setting out can be priced from a site visit when drawings are still in progress. The remaining scope is priced as drawings are issued, and each stage is confirmed in writing.

Are materials and plant included?

They can be. The contract sum can carry plant, materials and labour, with material selections and approvals confirmed before any order is placed.

How far do you travel for a project?

Site teams mobilise from the Dar es Salaam office. Travel and supervision costs are stated separately in the quotation, so the cost of working at your site is visible before award.

Send the drawings, the site location and the handover date. The office confirms a site visit and returns a measured quotation.
