Delivery model

How a project is run

The same sequence applies whether the scope is a single building or a multi-trade package.

01

Brief and site visit

Drawings or a written scope, the site and the handover date are reviewed together before pricing.

02

Measured quotation

Quantities, materials, plant and supervision are measured and issued with a programme.

03

Construction

Trades are run by registered supervisors, with weekly written progress and variation records.

04

Handover

Commissioning, as-built records and a defects schedule are issued with the building.

Questions

Project enquiries

Common questions before an award.

Can building, civil and electrical scope be awarded together?

Yes. The three registrations belong to the same company, so one appointment can carry structure, groundworks and power installation, with one programme and one supervisor.

Do you publish client names?

Client and project details are shared only with the client's consent. References are discussed during the enquiry rather than published online.

Can you price a project outside Dar es Salaam?

Yes. Site teams mobilise from the Dar es Salaam office, with travel, supervision and material logistics stated separately in the quotation.
