Common Technical Inquiries

What is your CRB registration and contractor classification?
+
Angoni Electrical Contractors is registered with the Contractors Registration Board of Tanzania as a Class 7 electrical contractor. Our registration number E7/0161/10/2002 certifies our qualification to undertake electrical contracting work across all sectors. This classification validates our capability to handle projects of any scale and complexity.
What geographical areas do you serve?
+
Our primary service area is Arusha and the Northern Tanzania region including Kilimanjaro and Manyara. For larger projects, we provide services across Tanzania depending on project scope and requirements. We have successfully completed projects in multiple regions and maintain the capability to deploy resources nationwide.
Do you provide emergency electrical services?
+
We provide 24/7 emergency electrical repair services for urgent situations including power outages, electrical faults, and safety hazards. Our emergency response team is available around the clock to address critical electrical issues that require immediate attention to minimize operational disruption and risk.
