What to send

Four Details That Speed Up a Quote.

  • Site location — town, plot or nearest landmark, plus access constraints for plant and deliveries.
  • Existing equipment — make, model and rating for generators, chillers or BTS power, with a photo of the nameplate if possible.
  • Drawings or scope — architectural, structural or electrical drawings, or a clear written scope if drawings do not exist yet.
  • Target dates — when the works must be complete and what cannot be interrupted during installation.
