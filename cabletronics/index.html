One contractor, four disciplines

Fewer Handovers. Clearer Accountability.

Critical facilities rarely fail because of one trade. They fail in the gaps between them — the plinth poured to the wrong level, the cable route no one owns, the commissioning that slips between suppliers.

Cabletronics closes those gaps by holding the mechanical, civil, building and electrical scopes under one contract. Interfaces are planned before mobilisation, then built and tested by the same team.

  • Single-contract delivery across four CRB-classified disciplines
  • Design coordination resolved on drawings, not discovered on site
  • Testing, commissioning and handover records included as standard
