01 / 05Standby power

Specialist Mechanical CRB Class 3

Industrial Generator Supply, Installation & Commissioning.

A turnkey engineering scope for plants and corporate campuses that cannot afford an outage: source the generating set, build the civil base, install the exhaust and fuel systems, wire the synchronised control panel, and prove the whole system under load.

Typical installations use heavy-duty diesel sets in the 500 kVA+ range, including Cummins and Perkins platforms, selected against the site's actual running load rather than its connected load.

License basisSPM3/0481/06/2010
Typical assets500 kVA+ gensets, ATS, sync panels
SectorsIndustrial plants, corporate offices
HandoverLoad test, records, operator briefing
  • Sourcing, delivery and site positioning
  • Concrete plinth, exhaust routing and fuel system
  • Synchronised control-panel wiring and changeover
  • Load testing, commissioning and zero-downtime cutover

02 / 05Civil works

Civil Works CRB Class 5

Municipal Infrastructure & Stormwater Drainage.

Local road maintenance, earthworks and drainage delivered as one sequence. We establish the sub-grade first, then build reinforced concrete box culverts and open channels sized to carry the seasonal flow away from the carriageway.

The result is a road that holds its profile through the rains, and a drainage line that can be inspected and maintained instead of failing silently under the surface.

License basisC5/1526/09/2023
Typical assetsBox culverts, open channels, sub-base
SectorsMunicipal roads, estates, industrial access
HandoverAs-built levels, compaction records
  • Earthworks, formation and foundational grading
  • Reinforced concrete box culverts and headwalls
  • Open drainage channels and kerbing
  • Sub-grade stabilisation and road structural maintenance
Discuss a civil scope

03 / 05Commercial cooling

Specialist Mechanical CRB Class 3

Commercial HVAC & Centralised Industrial Cooling.

Full-scope design, installation, ductwork routing and maintenance of centralised air-conditioning and industrial ventilation for corporate headquarters, banking halls and government facilities.

Plant is selected against the building's heat load and occupancy pattern, then coordinated with the electrical and structural packages so rooftop weight, condensate routes and power supplies are resolved before installation.

License basisSPM3/0481/06/2010
Typical assetsVRF systems, chillers, AHUs, ductwork
SectorsBanking halls, offices, government
HandoverCommissioning data, maintenance plan
  • Cooling-load review and VRF system design
  • Ductwork routing and industrial ventilation
  • Rooftop chiller and condenser installation
  • Controls integration and planned maintenance
Discuss an HVAC scope

04 / 05Building works

Building Works CRB Class 6

Multi-Storey Commercial & Residential Blocks.

Comprehensive structural management from foundation layout to concrete skeleton, masonry and plaster finishes for mid-sized corporate facilities and modern apartment complexes.

Structure, drainage and services are sequenced together. Setting-out, reinforcement and formwork are inspected before pours; masonry and plaster follow only once the frame has been accepted.

License basisB6/0580/09/2011
Typical assetsRC frames, slabs, masonry, plaster
SectorsCommercial blocks, apartments
HandoverAs-built drawings, finishes sign-off
  • Foundation layout, setting-out and substructure
  • Reinforced concrete columns, beams and slabs
  • Brickwork and blockwork masonry
  • Internal and external plastering finishes
Discuss a building scope

05 / 05Telecom power

Mechanical & Electrical CRB Class 3 / 7

Telecom BTS Site Power & Base Infrastructure.

Engineering deployment of hybrid power, heavy-duty solar backup arrays and smart lithium battery banks at base transceiver station sites — designed for network uptime in both rural and urban coverage areas.

Each site is treated as an energy system: grid availability, generator runtime, solar yield and battery depth of discharge are balanced before equipment is specified. Installation is planned around live network constraints.

License basisSPM3/0481/06/2010 · E7/0306/07/2007
Typical assetsHybrid controllers, solar arrays, Li-ion banks
SectorsTelecom towers, remote BTS compounds
HandoverSite test, monitoring, maintenance plan
  • Site power audit and hybrid system design
  • Heavy-duty solar backup array installation
  • Smart lithium battery banks and DC distribution
  • Commissioning and uptime-focused maintenance

Working together

Most Projects Need More Than One Line.

A building needs drainage. A generator needs a civil base. A BTS site needs both power and structure. That overlap is where our four registrations earn their keep.

A combined scope

One Programme, Not Three Contractors.

When scopes are split, the cost shows up later: a plinth cast before the genset is confirmed, a cable route that clashes with the drainage trench, a building handed over before the cooling plant is commissioned.

We plan those interfaces at tender stage and carry them through a single programme. The client keeps one line of communication and one set of handover records.
