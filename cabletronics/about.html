About the record

Reading a CRB Registration.

What the classes mean, and how to check them yourself.

Why does one company hold four registrations?

The CRB registers contractors by discipline, not by company. Mechanical, electrical, building and civil works each carry their own registration number and class, so a contractor must be listed separately for every category it intends to deliver.

What does the class number indicate?

The class is the CRB's grading of the contractor within that discipline. It is not a marketing label; it is a registration status that clients and tender committees can verify against the public register before awarding works.

What does “Local” mean on the register?

It is the CRB category for a locally registered contractor, as distinct from a foreign contractor registration. Cabletronics is listed under the Local category for all four entries.

How do I verify the registrations?

Each registration number above links to its entry on the Contractors Registration Board website. You can also search “Cabletronics” in the CRB contractor search and compare the class, category and address.
