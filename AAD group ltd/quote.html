What happens next

  • Scope review. We check the documents, confirm access and flag anything missing before pricing.
  • Site visit. For works around Arusha, an engineer visits to verify levels, ground and plant access.
  • Written quotation. Measured rates, programme, testing regime and a clear list of exclusions.
  • Clarification call. We walk through the figures with you and adjust the scope where needed.

    Before you send

    Details that speed up pricing.

    Drawings or bill of quantities

    Issued drawings with levels, typical sections and a priced or unpriced bill give the most accurate figure.

    Site access and ground

    Tell us about access roads, existing services, water for construction and any known rock or fill on site.

    Programme constraints

    Rainy season limits, occupied buildings, traffic restrictions and fixed completion dates all shape the method.
