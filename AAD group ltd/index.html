Site control

The programme holds when the ground is right.

Every project starts with levels, soil and access. We set the working platform, confirm the compaction standard and agree the sequence before plant arrives. This is the stage that protects later trades from lost days.

  • Setting out and level control by survey before excavation
  • Density and moisture testing on each compacted layer
  • Haul routes and stockpiles planned around the wet season
  • Traffic and pedestrian management written into the method statement

Supervision

An engineer stays with the work.

Each active location runs under a site engineer who owns the levels, the sequence and the daily record. Instructions reach the gang in the morning briefing, not through a chain of phone calls.

Sourcing is local where the specification allows. Aggregate, cement and reinforcement come through suppliers we have tested with, and plant is matched to the access the site actually has.

  • A site engineer assigned to every active location
  • Daily records of labour, plant and delivered materials
  • Weekly progress and cost reporting to the client
