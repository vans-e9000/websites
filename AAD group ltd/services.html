Services

Civil works from first cut to final surface.

Six work packages, each priced from measured quantities and delivered against a written method statement. We take on complete civil packages as well as single scopes inside a larger contract.

Roads Earthworks Drainage Water Structures External works
Infrastructure and building works Arusha base, plant and site teams
01 / Road works

Road construction and rehabilitation

We build and restore gravel and paved roads for district networks, industrial access and internal circulation. Work covers setting out, subgrade preparation, selected fill, base course, drainage crossings and surfacing.

  • Site clearance, setting out and level pegs
  • Cut and fill to design formation levels
  • Gravel base and bituminous surfacing
  • Shoulders, kerbs, crossings and road furniture
Price a road package
02 / Ground works

Earthworks and site formation

Bulk excavation and compaction for platforms, access roads and building footprints. We work to specified density and moisture content, with material testing on every lift and survey checks at each formation level.

  • Bulk and detailed excavation, rock removal
  • Cut and fill balancing with haul planning
  • Layer works with field density testing
  • Slope protection, erosion control and dewatering
Price earthworks
03 / Stormwater

Stormwater drainage and culverts

Arusha takes heavy seasonal rain. We build drainage that moves water away from structures and pavements: lined channels, pipe culverts, box culverts, catch pits and outfall structures with scour protection.

  • Open drains, lined channels and kerb inlets
  • Pipe and box culverts with headwalls
  • Catch pits, manholes and gully connections
  • Outfalls, aprons and scour protection
Price drainage works
04 / Water

Water supply and reticulation

Trenching, pipe laying, jointing and pressure testing for distribution mains and service connections, plus the concrete chambers and supports around them. Work is programmed around supply hours and street access.

  • Trench excavation, bedding and backfill
  • HDPE, uPVC and galvanised pipe laying
  • Jointing, chlorination and pressure testing
  • Chambers, valve boxes and surface reinstatement
Price water works
05 / Structures

Reinforced concrete structures

Foundations, columns, beams, suspended slabs, retaining walls and water-retaining structures cast to drawing. Formwork and reinforcement are inspected before every pour, with cube tests recorded against the specified class.

  • Strip, pad and raft foundations
  • Columns, beams and suspended slabs
  • Retaining, boundary and basement walls
  • Water tanks, reservoirs and pump bases
Price structural works
06 / External works

Site servicing and external works

The works that make a completed building usable: access roads, parking, hardstanding, boundary walls, drainage connections and yard levels. We coordinate these with the building trades so the site is handed over in one sequence.

  • Access roads, parking bays and paving
  • Hardstanding, yard slabs and kerbing
  • Boundary walls, gates and fencing
  • Utility trenches, connections and final levels
Price external works
Method

What comes with every package.

Written method statements, tested materials and a named engineer for the duration of the works.

Method statement

Sequence, plant, temporary works, traffic management and hold points issued before mobilisation.

Material testing

Compaction, concrete cube and pipe pressure results recorded and shared with the client file.

Progress reporting

Weekly output against programme, with quantities, labour and plant listed on one page.

Safety controls

Daily briefings, personal protective equipment, excavation support and plant exclusion zones.

Measured pricing

Rates built from quantities, haul distance and plant hours, so variations are easy to value.

Handover file

As-built drawings, test certificates, defects schedule and maintenance notes at completion.

Scope review

Tell us what the drawings require.

Send the bill of quantities or the drawing set with the site location. We confirm feasibility, plant access and the testing regime before we quote.
