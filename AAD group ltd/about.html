About the company

A civil contractor built around site control.

AAD Group (T) Limited is a locally owned civils and building contractor based in Arusha. We hold three registrations with the Contractors Registration Board of Tanzania: Specialist Civil at Class 2, Civil at Class 5 and Building at Class 5, and we deliver works for public bodies, developers and private clients.

Who we are

Registered in 2025, run by people who have built on site.

AAD Group (T) Limited was registered with the Contractors Registration Board in May 2025 under three entries: Specialist Civil at Class 2 (SPC2/315/05/2025), Civil at Class 5 (C5/1874/05/2025) and Building at Class 5 (B5/1944/05/2025). All three are local contractor registrations held from Arusha City.

Our scope is civil: roads and pavements, earthworks, drainage, water reticulation and reinforced concrete. We keep the trades that decide the quality of those works under direct supervision, which means the same team sets the levels, inspects the reinforcement and signs off the compacted layer.

Managing Director Debora Gombela leads the company, with estimating and site management handled from the Arusha office at P.O. Box 1062.

Standards

Decisions recorded, not remembered.

Civil works fail at the layers you cannot see: the compaction under a slab, the bedding under a pipe, the cover to reinforcement. We hold inspection points at each of those stages and record the result before the next trade covers the work.

  • Inspection and test plan agreed with the client before mobilisation
  • Compaction and concrete cube results filed against each location
  • Daily safety briefing and plant inspection before work starts
  • Written instruction for every change to scope or sequence
Plant and supply

Plant matched to the access, materials matched to the test.

Excavation, haulage and compaction plant is selected against the site access and the volumes in the programme. On constrained urban plots we work in smaller passes with smaller machines, which keeps the programme realistic.

Aggregate, cement, reinforcement and pipes are sourced through suppliers we have tested with. Where the specification allows local materials, we use them and test them to the same standard as imported alternatives.
