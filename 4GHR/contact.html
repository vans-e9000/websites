Contact questions

Before you send the brief.

How quickly will I hear back?

Every project brief is reviewed within one working day. If the scope needs a site visit, the first reply confirms the visit date and what to prepare.

Can I send drawings after the brief?

Yes. Send the brief first, then reply to the email with drawings, schedules, or photographs. The scope is updated when the information arrives.

Does 4GHR work outside Dar es Salaam?

Yes. Projects elsewhere in Tanzania are priced with mobilisation, travel, and delivery costs stated separately.

What if the scope is not defined yet?

Send the project stage and the decision you need to make. The first response defines the information required before a price is issued.

Can I call instead of sending the form?

Yes. Call +255 754 711 801 or email ceo_4ghri@outlook.com if the project information is already available.
