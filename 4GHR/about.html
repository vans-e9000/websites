Working principles

Four habits that hold a package together.

The way the company works is consistent across every discipline and every project size.

01

Listen first

Site conditions, building use, and the decisions already made are reviewed before a price is offered.

02

Make scope visible

Inclusions, exclusions, interfaces, and assumptions are written into the priced scope in plain language.

03

Communicate directly

One accountable contact answers questions and records the decisions that change the work.

04

Close with a record

Test results, as-built notes, and outstanding items are issued as part of handover.
