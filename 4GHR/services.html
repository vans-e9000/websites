Electrical works

4GHR installs the electrical systems that make a building usable: distribution boards, sub-mains, final circuits, lighting, containment, earthing, and the testing that proves the installation.

Work is coordinated with the structural and services trades from the first site visit, so routes, penetrations, and access are agreed before containment goes in.

  • Power distribution and sub-main installation
  • Internal and external lighting systems
  • Cable containment, trunking, and support systems
  • Earthing, bonding, and protection checks
  • Testing, commissioning support, and handover records

Specialist electrical

Specialist packages carry more technical risk than a standard fit-out. 4GHR takes on control panels, protection systems, low-voltage installations, and equipment connections where design intent, testing, and documentation need to be held together.

The package is reviewed against the drawings before mobilisation. Any gap between design and site conditions is raised while it can still be resolved on paper.

  • Control and distribution panel installation
  • Protection, metering, and low-voltage systems
  • Equipment power connections and isolation
  • Factory and site acceptance test support
  • Technical records for commissioning and handover

Civil contracting

Civil works prepare the ground and the structure for everything that follows. 4GHR delivers site clearance, excavation, concrete works, drainage, access, and external works for commercial and institutional projects.

Sequencing is set with the electrical programme where trenches, ducts, and equipment bases share the same ground.

  • Site preparation and clearance
  • Excavation, backfill, and compaction
  • Concrete foundations, bases, and slabs
  • Drainage, ducts, and service trenches
  • Access roads, hardstanding, and external works

Service questions

Choose the right starting point.

How do I know which service applies?

Send the project location, a short description of the work, and any drawings or photographs available. The first review confirms whether electrical, specialist electrical, or civil should lead, or whether the scope should be combined.

Can electrical and civil work be priced together?

Yes. When the workstreams share trenches, access, or a commissioning date, a combined package makes the interfaces and the programme easier to control.

What should a request for pricing include?

Include the scope, drawings or specifications, quantities where available, site location, programme dates, procurement status, and any condition that affects access or working hours.

Who tests and commissions the electrical installation?

4GHR carries out the testing defined in the scope and supports third-party commissioning where the project requires it. Results and outstanding items are issued at handover.
