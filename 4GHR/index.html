How we work

The scope is settled before the site gets busy.

Every package starts with a review of drawings, site conditions, programme, and the decisions that still sit open. We price and plan against the information that exists, and we record what changes.

That review happens before materials are ordered and before crews are committed, which keeps the programme honest from the first week.

  • Single point of contact. The managing director stays accountable for scope, programme, and close-out.
  • Interfaces settled early. Containment, trenches, equipment access, and other trades are coordinated before work starts.
  • Records that survive handover. Test results, as-built notes, and outstanding items are compiled as the work progresses.

Common questions

Before the first site visit.

Which work can 4GHR take on?

Registered scope covers electrical works, specialist electrical works, and civil contracting. A single project can combine the three where the interfaces need one team.

Where does 4GHR operate?

The company works from Dar es Salaam and takes projects across Tanzania.

How does a project brief work?

Send the location, scope, drawings or notes, and programme. We review the information and reply within one working day with the next step or a request for missing detail.

Can 4GHR join a project that is already on site?

Yes, where access, documentation, and the remaining scope can be assessed. Share the current position and we will confirm what can be taken on.

What happens at handover?

Completed works are handed over with test results, as-built notes, and a list of outstanding items where applicable.
