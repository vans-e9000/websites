How we run a project

A four-stage process from first consultation through handover, the same on every project regardless of size or class.

01

Consultation

We start with a detailed discussion of your requirements, budget, and timeline, so the quotation reflects the project you actually need.

02

Planning

We prepare the design, engineering, permits, and schedule, and confirm the project scope and milestones before mobilization.

03

Construction

We build with specified materials, a supervised site team, and documented inspections at each phase, keeping the work on schedule and to specification.

04

Handover

We complete a full inspection, hand over the documentation, and walk the client through the finished project before closeout.
