What we do

Our Services

Civil engineering, building construction, project management, and infrastructure services, each listed with the project class and scope we handle under it.

Class 6

Civil Engineering

Civil engineering under our Class 6 registration, C6/3298/05/2026. We handle road construction, bridge engineering, drainage systems, water supply infrastructure, and earthworks, with supervised site work from mobilization through handover.

  • Road construction and rehabilitation with durable, high-quality materials
  • Bridge engineering and structural assessment for safe river crossings
  • Storm water drainage systems to manage urban flooding
  • Water supply infrastructure including pipelines and treatment facilities
  • Site preparation and earthworks with precision grading
  • Retaining walls and soil stabilization for challenging terrain
  • Utility infrastructure installation and maintenance

We follow Tanzanian engineering standards, keep the site organized, and document progress as each civil engineering project advances.

Scope under Class 6

Roads, bridges, drainage, water supply, earthworks, and utility infrastructure, supervised and documented under our Class 6 CRB registration.

Registration: C6/3298/05/2026
Authority: Contractors Registration Board, Tanzania
Class 7

Building Construction

Building construction under our Class 7 registration, B7/4917/03/2026. We carry commercial and residential buildings from foundation and structural work through finishes and handover, to agreed specifications and Tanzanian building requirements.

  • Commercial building construction for offices, retail spaces, and mixed-use developments
  • Residential housing developments from single-family homes to apartment complexes
  • Industrial facilities including warehouses, factories, and processing plants
  • Renovation and retrofitting of existing structures for modern standards
  • Interior finishing and fit-out with premium materials and craftsmanship
  • Structural reinforcement and seismic upgrading for enhanced safety
  • Specialized institutional buildings for healthcare, education, and government

We use specified materials, organized site teams, and documented inspections so each building phase is checked before the next one starts.

Scope under Class 7

Commercial, residential, and industrial buildings, plus renovation and institutional work, executed under our Class 7 CRB registration.

Registration: B7/4917/03/2026
Authority: Contractors Registration Board, Tanzania
Services

Project Management

Project management from first consultation through completion. We coordinate scope, sequence, resources, supervisors, and client reporting so each project stays organized around agreed deadlines and specifications. Each project has a named contact for day-to-day questions.

  • Project planning and scheduling with detailed timelines and milestones
  • Budget management and cost control with transparent financial reporting
  • Quality assurance and inspection at every construction phase
  • Stakeholder coordination with government agencies, contractors, and clients
  • Risk management and mitigation planning for construction challenges
  • Health and safety oversight with documented site safety protocols
  • Procurement and supply chain management for materials and equipment
  • Regulatory compliance and permit management

We keep clients informed with scheduled progress reports, site visits, and a named contact for day-to-day questions on each project.

What we manage

Schedule, budget, quality, safety, procurement, and stakeholder relationships, so the client has one accountable team across the project.

Client contact: One named lead per project
Reporting: Scheduled progress reports and site visits
Services

Infrastructure Development

Infrastructure that supports connectivity, water management, and community access across Tanzania. We plan and build public and private infrastructure in Dar es Salaam and surrounding regions, and we complete each project to its agreed purpose.

  • Urban infrastructure development including streetscapes and public spaces
  • Rural access roads connecting communities to markets and services
  • Public facilities construction including schools, clinics, and community centers
  • Utility infrastructure including water, sewage, and electrical systems
  • Community development projects aligned with local needs and priorities
  • Maintenance and upgrades of existing infrastructure assets
  • Sustainable infrastructure incorporating environmental considerations

We work with the client, supervising engineers, and local contractors to match each infrastructure project to its agreed purpose and location.

Infrastructure focus

Streetscapes, rural access roads, public facilities, utility networks, and community infrastructure — planned and built for the communities they serve.

Regions: Dar es Salaam and surrounding regions
Approach: Coordinated with the client and supervising engineers
