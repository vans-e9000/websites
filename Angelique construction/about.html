Who we are

About Us

Civil and building contractor registered with Tanzania's Contractors Registration Board, operating from Makongo Ward, Dar es Salaam.

Our Story

Angelique Construction Company Limited is a registered Tanzanian contractor specializing in civil engineering and building construction. We hold Class 6 registration C6/3298/05/2026 and Class 7 registration B7/4917/03/2026 from the Contractors Registration Board, which list the project classes we are authorized to undertake.

We operate from Makongo Ward, Dar es Salaam, and take on civil engineering and building work for public and private clients. Each project is supervised and documented under the applicable CRB registration, with the client receiving a single point of contact throughout.

Under the leadership of Managing Director Beatrice Mbunda, the company runs its civil engineering and building operations from Dar es Salaam, with direct client contact and supervised site execution on every project we take on.
