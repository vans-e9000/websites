Process

Three steps to a finished structure

Tender & planning

We review drawings, measure the works and return a priced bill of quantities with a realistic programme, with no vague allowances.

Build & supervise

Agree mobilisation timing, site supervision and progress updates as part of the project scope.

Inspect & hand over

Joint snag inspection, documented defect rectification, then full turnover of as-built records and warranties.
