About the company

A local contractor built around supervision.

Continental Tech Solutions Limited is a Tanzanian contractor registered in building, civil and specialist mechanical works, with its head office in Mwanza and project teams working across the country.

The company

One contract, one accountable team.

The company contracts building, civil and mechanical packages under a single responsibility. A client dealing with one contractor avoids the gaps that open between a structural frame, a drainage line and a services riser, because the same site team plans all three.

Work is priced from drawings and specifications, built to the approved revisions, and recorded as it proceeds. The Managing Director, Joseph Makondo, signs each contract and remains the client's contact for commercial decisions through to handover.

  • Head office in Mwanza, project teams nationwide
  • Building, civil and specialist mechanical registrations
  • Directly employed site supervision
  • Written quotations with quantities and exclusions

Organisation

Who does what on a project.

Three functions carry a project from quotation to defects period. Each one has a named person the client can reach directly.

Head office

Runs contracts, procurement and accounts from Room No. 96, CCM Mkoa Building in Mwanza. Quotations, contract documents and payment certificates are issued from here.

Site supervision

A resident engineer or site supervisor runs the works, keeps the day book, checks setting out and holds each stage for inspection before the next trade starts.

Commercial and procurement

Prepares quantities, compares supplier quotations, schedules deliveries and tracks variations against the contract sum before work is instructed.

Safety

Site rules apply to everyone, including visitors.

Safety inductions run before a worker starts on site. The supervisor records attendance, issues the protective equipment required for the task, and stops work where an activity cannot proceed safely.

  • Helmets, boots and high-visibility vests on every site
  • Edge protection and inspected access before work at height
  • Excavations supported and barricaded
  • Toolbox talks recorded weekly
  • Incident and near-miss register kept on site

Quality

Hold points that cannot be skipped.

Quality control is built into the programme as hold points. Work does not continue past a hold point until the check is signed by the supervisor and, where the contract requires it, the consultant.

Our hold points cover setting out, reinforcement before each pour, formwork and props before loading, drainage falls before backfill, pressure checks on pipework and commissioning of mechanical systems.

  • Setting out checked against approved grid lines
  • Reinforcement checked against the bar bending schedule
  • Concrete cubes taken for each pour and results filed
  • Pressure and flow records before ceilings close

The people

Supervision that stays on site.

Our supervisors work where the work happens. They arrive before the first trade and leave after the last, hold the day book, and report progress in writing each week.

Crews are made up of directly employed foremen and locally engaged tradespeople, with plant hired for the duration of the activity rather than for the whole programme.
