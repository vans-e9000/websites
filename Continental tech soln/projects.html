Records

Documentation runs alongside the works.

A project is only complete when the client can operate what we built. Our site team compiles the record set as work proceeds, so handover does not depend on reconstructing information at the end.

  • Approved drawings and instruction register
  • Concrete pour cards and reinforcement checks
  • Delivery notes for cement, steel and pipework
  • Pressure and flow records for mechanical systems
  • As-built drawings and equipment manuals
