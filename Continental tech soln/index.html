Urban delivery

City plots need a programme built around access.

On constrained sites we plan material deliveries outside peak traffic, sequence concrete pours with ready-mix suppliers, and keep hoist and crane movements inside agreed hours. The site supervisor records daily progress against the programme and issues a weekly report to the client and consultant.

Where a plot has no laydown area, materials are scheduled to arrive in sequence rather than stockpiled. That decision sits in the programme before mobilisation, not on the day of delivery.

Delivery

From site visit to defects period.

Every project follows the same four stages. Each stage has a named responsible person and a document the client receives.

  1. 01

    Site assessment and pricing

    We visit the site, review drawings and ground information, and price the scope we can see. The quotation lists quantities, material specifications and the construction programme.

  2. 02

    Mobilisation and registration

    Plant, labour and materials move to site within the agreed mobilisation period. The project is registered with the Contractors Registration Board before works begin.

  3. 03

    Construction and supervision

    A resident engineer or site supervisor runs daily works. Materials verification, progress records and safety checks are compiled weekly and shared with the client.

  4. 04

    Handover and defects liability

    We close out snagging, hand over as-built drawings and equipment manuals, and attend to defects for the period stated in the contract.
