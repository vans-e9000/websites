01 / Building

Building construction

We build commercial and residential structures from setting out to handover. The building team takes responsibility for the frame, envelope and finishes, and coordinates the mechanical installation so that sleeves, risers and plant spaces are built in at the right stage.

Every structure starts with a reviewed set of drawings and a method statement for excavation, concrete and lifting. Where drawings conflict, we raise the query in writing before the work is built.

  • Reinforced concrete frames and suspended slabs
  • Blockwork, plaster, tiling and painting
  • Roofing, doors, windows and ironmongery
  • External works, boundary walls and paving

02 / Civil

Civil works

Civil crews handle the ground and the movement of water. That covers access roads, parking, storm water drainage, culverts and water supply lines for developments, institutions and industrial yards.

Works are set out from approved levels and checked against the design before excavation. Compaction and layer thickness are recorded for each pavement section, and drainage falls are verified before backfill.

  • Site clearance, excavation and fill
  • Sub-base and base pavement layers
  • Storm water drains, culverts and kerbs
  • Water supply and sewer reticulation
Price civil works

03 / Specialist mechanical

Specialist mechanical installations

Our Class 3 specialist mechanical registration covers the wet services and ventilation inside a building. We install plumbing, drainage, water supply, ductwork and plant, then commission each system and hand over the operating documents.

Installation follows coordinated services drawings. Pipe routes, duct sizes and plant clearances are agreed with the building team before first fix, which reduces cutting and rework once finishes begin.

  • Cold and hot water plumbing
  • Soil, waste and vent drainage
  • Supply and extract ventilation ducting
  • Pumps, tanks, plant rooms and commissioning

Every contract

What the client receives.

The four commitments below apply to every project, regardless of size or scope.

A supervised site

A named site supervisor or resident engineer is on site for the working day. Attendance, labour returns and plant hours are recorded daily.

Weekly reporting

Progress against programme, materials delivered, instructions received and safety observations reach the client every week in one report.

Materials with records

Cement, reinforcement and pipework arrive with delivery notes. Reinforcement is checked against the bar bending schedule before concrete is placed.

Handover documents

As-built drawings, equipment manuals, commissioning records and a defects schedule are issued at completion.

Process

Five stages, defined before mobilisation.

Each stage closes with a document the client keeps. No stage begins before the previous one is signed off.

  1. 01

    Brief and site visit

    We review the drawings, visit the site and confirm access, ground conditions and available services.

  2. 02

    Quotation and programme

    The quotation states quantities, specifications, exclusions and a programme with milestone dates.

  3. 03

    Contract and registration

    Contract documents are signed and the project is registered with the Contractors Registration Board.

  4. 04

    Construction and inspection

    Works proceed against the approved drawings with weekly reports and stage inspections by the consultant.

  5. 05

    Handover and defects period

    Snagging is closed, documents are issued and the defects liability period in the contract begins.

Questions

Contract and scope.

Which contract values can Continental Tech Solutions take?

Building and civil works are registered at Class 6 and specialist mechanical works at Class 3. The Contractors Registration Board sets the contract value range for each class, and we price work inside the range that matches the registration held for that scope.

Can one contract cover building and mechanical works?

Yes. Holding building, civil and specialist mechanical registrations lets us contract the structure and the building services together, with one programme and one site supervisor responsible for the interfaces.

Do you work outside Mwanza?

Project teams mobilise from the Mwanza head office to sites across Tanzania. Local labour and suppliers are engaged on each site, and the site supervisor stays resident for the duration of the works.

What do you need to prepare a quotation?

Architectural and structural drawings, a bill of quantities where one exists, the site location and the intended start date. For renovation work, we also need photographs or a site visit to confirm the existing condition.

Next step

Scope your package with the site team.

Send the drawings and the site location. We confirm the scope, the class of registration required and the programme before pricing.
