Pricing

How the quotation is built.

Rates are built from measured quantities, current supplier prices and the labour and plant needed for the programme.

  1. 01

    Measured quantities

    Excavation, concrete, reinforcement, blockwork, finishes and pipework are measured from the drawings rather than estimated from floor area.

  2. 02

    Current material prices

    Cement, steel, aggregates, pipework and fittings are priced from supplier quotations valid for the tender period.

  3. 03

    Labour, plant and programme

    Crew size, supervision and plant hire follow from the works programme, including any phasing or night work the site requires.

  4. 04

    Exclusions stated in writing

    The quotation names what is excluded, such as authority fees, service connections or client-supplied materials, so the contract sum is unambiguous.

Questions

Before you send.

How long does a quotation take?

Small and medium scopes with complete drawings are priced within five working days of the site visit. Larger or phased packages take longer, and we confirm the date when the request is received.

Can you price from a bill of quantities only?

Yes. Send the bill of quantities with the drawings it refers to. We price each item and state any quantity or specification we believe needs confirming before award.

Do you charge for the site visit?

Site visits in Mwanza are free of charge for projects we are invited to price. Visits elsewhere in Tanzania are free where the project proceeds to quotation, and travel is otherwise agreed in advance.

Can the works be phased around an operating building?

Yes. Phasing, night work and service shutdowns are planned with the client and written into the programme before mobilisation.
