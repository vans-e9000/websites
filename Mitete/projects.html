Sector coverage

Clients we work with

We work with district councils, religious organisations, schools, health facilities, cooperatives and private developers. Contracts are executed under written agreement with a measured bill of quantities.

  • Local government and public institutions procuring through tender.
  • Faith based organisations and school boards building on donated land.
  • Private developers building homes, rentals and small commercial premises.
  • Cooperatives and agribusinesses requiring stores, sheds and access works.
Reporting

How progress reaches you

Each week you receive one report, not a folder of photographs. It states what moved, what it cost and what needs a decision.

  • Work completed against the agreed programme, with the next two weeks scheduled.
  • Quantities measured and valued, matching the interim valuation.
  • Photographs of active fronts, labelled by element rather than by date.
  • Materials delivered and consumed, with delivery notes attached.
  • Labour and plant returns, plus any standing time explained.
  • Risks, delays and the decisions we need from you, dated.
