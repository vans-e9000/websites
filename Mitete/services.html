Service detail

What each service includes

Building construction

Buildings that meet the drawings and the budget

We build residential houses, classroom blocks, health facilities, offices and small commercial buildings. Work starts with setting out from the approved drawings and continues through foundations, frame, envelope and finishes, with a materials submittal for every element before it is installed.

  • Excavation, hardcore, blinding, footings and reinforced concrete ground beams.
  • Columns, beams, suspended slabs and staircases cast to the structural design.
  • Blockwork, roofing, ceiling, plaster, floor finishes, doors, windows and paint.
  • Electrical conduit, water supply, sanitary installation and septic systems.
Price a building project
Roads and earthworks

Roads that stay open through the rain season

Our road crews build and upgrade district roads, feeder roads and site access. The work is led by drainage design, since water is what takes a gravel road out of service. Camber, side drains and crossing structures are set before the wearing course is placed.

  • Clearing, grubbing, stripping and formation to surveyed alignment and levels.
  • Compaction in layers with density checks recorded per chainage.
  • Gravel wearing course, camber, mitre drains and lined side drains.
  • Pipe and box culverts, drifts and reinforced crossings at water courses.
Price road works
Water and sanitation

Water storage and supply built to hold

We construct the civil works around water supply: tank bases, pump houses, valve chambers, pipeline trenches and distribution lines. Every tank base is cast level and cured before the tank is lifted, and every line is pressure tested before trenches are backfilled.

  • Reinforced concrete plinths and ring beams for tanks from 10,000 to 100,000 litres.
  • Trenching, bedding, pipe laying, jointing and thrust blocks on gravity lines.
  • Pump houses, meter chambers, tap stands and livestock troughs.
  • Institutional sanitation blocks with septic tanks and soakaway pits.
Price water works
Drainage and flood control

Storm water handled before it reaches your building

The regions we build in receive intense seasonal rain. We size and build drainage that moves water away from foundations, roads and yards: lined channels, culverts, grated inlets and retaining walls on sloping ground.

  • Concrete lined drains, u-channels, kerbs and grated inlet boxes.
  • Box and pipe culverts with headwalls, wing walls and apron protection.
  • Reinforced retaining walls and gabion protection on cut slopes.
  • Soak pits, French drains and surface water falls at building perimeters.
Price drainage works
Renovation and repair

Repairs that extend the life of a working building

We repair and refurbish occupied buildings without shutting them down. Roofs, ceilings, floors, wet areas and structural cracks are assessed first, then repaired in phases that keep the building in use.

  • Crack diagnosis, stitching, re-plastering and structural strengthening.
  • Roof sheet replacement, truss repair, fascia and rainwater goods.
  • Damp treatment, tanking, floor screeds and tile replacement.
  • Kitchen, bathroom and plumbing upgrades for schools, clinics and offices.
Price a renovation
Site supervision

Technical control for clients running their own works

Some clients hold materials and labour and need engineering control on site. We provide supervision, setting out, testing and reporting so the work meets the drawings and the specification.

  • Setting out, level transfer and as-built measurement of completed work.
  • Concrete cube sampling, slump checks and compaction testing records.
  • Daily labour and plant returns, plus weekly cost against budget.
  • Snag lists, inspection checklists and handover documentation.

Commercial terms

How quotes and payments are structured

Every quote states quantities, rates and assumptions. Nothing is left to interpretation at final account.

  • Measured bill of quantities with unit rates for labour, material and plant.
  • Interim valuations against work executed, reviewed on site before certification.
  • Retention held to the agreed percentage and released after the defects period.
  • Variations priced and signed before execution, with the programme impact stated.
