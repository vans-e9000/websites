About the company

A Tanzanian contractor built around site control

Mitete Construction and Civil Works Enterprises operates from its own office and yard in western Tanzania. The company holds a Specialist Civil registration in Class 3 from the Contractors Registration Board and delivers building, road, water and drainage projects for public and private clients.

Our work

Construction managed by the people who build it

The company was registered to serve a simple gap in the region: clients could find labour, but few contractors could show measured quantities, tested materials and a programme that held through the rain season. Mitete was set up to close that gap with direct supervision and written records.

Managing Director Juma Mwanakatwe leads the company from its western Tanzania base. Site agents run each project with a foreman, a storekeeper and a survey assistant. Materials are ordered against the approved schedule, and every structural element is inspected before the next stage begins.

The company operates within the registration categories granted by the Contractors Registration Board and prices work against the scope it is registered to execute. Where a project falls outside that scope, we say so before a quotation is issued.

Safety and environment

Every shift starts with a briefing

Excavations, working at height and material handling carry the highest risk on our sites. Each is planned before work begins and briefed to the crew at the start of the shift.

  • Helmets, boots and high visibility vests issued and worn on every active site.
  • Excavations battered or supported, with barricades and lighting at night.
  • Dust suppression on haul routes and spoil placed clear of water courses.
  • Site clearance and reinstatement agreed with the client before demobilisation.
Plant and people

Equipment maintained in house, trades trained on site

Plant and equipment

Excavators, a backhoe loader, tipper trucks, plate compactors and concrete mixers are held by the company and maintained between projects by our own fitter. Service records are kept against each unit, and hire rates are stated separately in every bill so you can see what is owned and what is hired.

Training and local employment

Trades are recruited from the project area. General labour is inducted on the first morning, and skilled hands work under a foreman who signs off each element. Apprentices from local technical colleges join for the duration of a project and rotate through masonry, steel fixing and setting out.
