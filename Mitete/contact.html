Before you call

Bring these to the first meeting

A short meeting with the right documents turns into a firm quotation faster. If you do not hold drawings, bring photographs and the site measurements instead.

  • Architectural and structural drawings, including the site plan.
  • Title deed, lease or a letter confirming the client owns the site.
  • Any geotechnical report, survey data or previous bill of quantities.
  • A budget range and the date you need the works completed.
