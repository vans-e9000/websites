How we work

From first site visit to handover

STEP 01

Site assessment

We walk the site with your drawings, check access, levels, soil conditions and water availability, then confirm what the work requires.

STEP 02

Priced bill of quantities

You receive measured quantities, unit rates, a materials schedule and a programme with clear assumptions and exclusions.

STEP 03

Mobilisation and construction

Crews, plant and materials move to site against a signed programme. Supervisors record daily output and test results.

STEP 04

Inspection and handover

Completed works are inspected with the client, defects are closed out, and you receive as-built drawings plus maintenance notes.

Plant and equipment

Plant that keeps work moving

Earthmoving

Excavators and a backhoe loader for foundations, trenching and bulk cut and fill.

Compaction

Plate compactors and rollers for layers that hold their density through the wet season.

Haulage

Tipper trucks moving spoil, gravel and materials without waiting on hired transport.

Concrete works

Mixers, vibrators and formwork sets for structural pours cast to a stated class.

Where a scope needs a grader, crane or heavy roller, the unit is hired at a fixed rate and shown as a separate line in the quotation, so the price stays honest about what is owned and what is brought in.

Assurance

Controls that protect your budget and your programme

Construction risk on the projects we build comes from rain, haul distance and material supply. Our planning responds to all three before work starts.

  • Rain season planning with drainage and stockpile protection built into the programme.
  • Material orders placed against tested samples, with delivery records matched to the bill.
  • Variations confirmed in writing before the work is executed, so final accounts hold no surprises.
  • Working at height, excavation and lifting procedures briefed at the start of every shift.
