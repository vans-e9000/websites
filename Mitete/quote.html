Pricing questions

What clients ask before awarding work

How is a quotation prepared?

From measured quantities taken off your drawings, priced at current material and labour rates for the district where the site sits. Transport, camp and water costs appear as separate items when the site is outside the home region.

Are site visits charged?

Site visits within the home region are free. For sites further afield we quote a fixed visit cost and deduct it from the contract sum if the project is awarded to us.

Can work start before drawings are complete?

Early works such as clearing, access and setting out can start on a sketch layout. Structural work requires stamped drawings, since we build to a design we can certify.

How are payments scheduled?

Interim valuations are raised against work executed, usually monthly, with retention held to the agreed percentage. The schedule is written into the contract before mobilisation.

Do you supply materials?

Yes. Cement, reinforcement, aggregates, pipes and roofing are supplied against approved samples and delivery notes. Client supplied materials are accepted against a joint condition record.

What happens after handover?

A six month defects period applies to completed works. Reported defects are attended within five working days, and as-built drawings plus maintenance notes are issued at handover.
