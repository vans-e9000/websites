The job behind the contact details

What you are actually contacting us about.

This page is not just a phone number. It is the point where a plot becoming a building, a wall, a slab, a drainage line or a patched road turns into a quoted job with a registered contractor on it. Everything below is the work that sits behind these contact details, so when you write to us or call, you are not starting from zero.

For the site contact

If you have the keys to the plot, you are already part of the job.

Send the plot location and town, the category of work you need, any drawings or bill of quantities you already have, and the programme dates you are working to. If you can also say whether the ground is bare, whether someone has already dug, or whether materials are on site, we can usually return a complete written quotation faster — and tell you upfront where the unknowns are.

What the work looks like

Tell us what stage it is at.

If you already know where your job sits — just starting, mid-build, scaffolding, finishes — send that with the plot details. These are the kinds of sites we quote for, so a photo or description of the stage helps us return a complete written quotation faster.
