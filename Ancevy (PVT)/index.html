How we work

From site assessment to handover in four clear stages.

Each stage has a defined output, so you always know what has been agreed, what is being installed and what has been tested.

01

Site assessment

We review the supply point, existing load, building plans and programme constraints, then confirm what the installation needs to carry today and in the next phase.

02

Design and quotation

You receive single-line diagrams, a bill of quantities, cable and protection schedules, and a fixed-price quotation for the agreed scope.

03

Installation

Our teams coordinate with civil, mechanical and finishing trades, work to staged inspection points and keep a daily record of progress on site.

04

Testing and handover

Every circuit is tested and documented. We hand over as-built drawings, test sheets and operating guidance, then remain available for maintenance.

Questions

Practical answers before you commit.

If your question is not covered here, call the office or send a short brief and we will answer directly.

What size of electrical project does ANCEVY take on?

Our Class 4 registration covers residential, commercial and industrial installations. We deliver single-building fit-outs, multi-block developments and plant power distribution, and we take maintenance contracts for completed installations.

Do you work outside Mwanza?

Yes. We run projects in Dar es Salaam, Arusha, Dodoma and Iringa, and travel to sites elsewhere in Tanzania when the scope supports a dedicated team. Travel and accommodation are stated in the quotation, not added later.

How is a project priced?

We price from a site assessment and a defined bill of quantities. You receive a fixed price for the agreed scope, with cable sizes, protection ratings and equipment models listed. Any variation is agreed in writing before the work proceeds.

Are your installations tested and documented?

Every completed circuit is tested for insulation resistance, earth continuity, polarity and earth loop impedance. Results are recorded on test sheets and issued with as-built drawings at handover, alongside operating guidance for the equipment installed.

How quickly can I get a quotation?

We acknowledge every enquiry within one business day and issue a quotation within two to five working days after the site assessment, depending on the size of the scope. Urgent breakdowns are triaged by phone on the same day.

Do you maintain installations you did not build?

Yes. We start with a condition inspection, record defects and prioritise them by safety and operational risk. From there we can provide planned maintenance or correct the findings as a one-off works order.

Ready to scope your next electrical project?

Send drawings, a site address or a short brief. We review the scope and respond with a quotation within one business day.
