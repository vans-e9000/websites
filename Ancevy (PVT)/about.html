About ANCEVY

ANCEVY (PVT) LIMITED is a fully registered Class 4 electrical contractor based in Mwanza, Tanzania. We deliver professional electrical solutions across residential, commercial, and industrial sectors with a commitment to quality, safety, and reliability.

Company Overview

Our commitment to quality electrical services across Tanzania.

Our Mission

To provide reliable, safe, and high-quality electrical solutions that meet the growing infrastructure needs of Tanzania. We focus on delivering exceptional workmanship, adhering to safety standards, and building long-term relationships with our clients through professional service delivery and continuous improvement. Our mission extends beyond simply completing projects - we aim to contribute to Tanzania's development by ensuring electrical infrastructure is built to last and operates safely and efficiently.

Our Core Values

  • Quality Excellence: We deliver work that meets and exceeds industry standards. Every installation, every connection, and every project is executed with precision and attention to detail. We believe that quality is not negotiable and that our clients deserve nothing less than the best.
  • Safety First: Electrical safety is paramount in every project we undertake. We implement comprehensive safety protocols, provide proper training to our team, and follow all regulatory safety requirements. We never compromise on safety for the sake of speed or cost.
  • Integrity & Transparency: We conduct business with transparency and honesty. Our clients can trust that we will communicate clearly about project progress, challenges, and solutions. We believe in building relationships based on trust and mutual respect.
  • Reliability & Consistency: Our clients count on us to deliver on time and within budget. We honor our commitments and maintain consistent quality across all projects. When we say we will do something, we follow through.
  • Professionalism: We maintain high standards in all our interactions and work. From initial consultation to project completion and beyond, our team conducts itself with professionalism, courtesy, and respect for our clients and their properties.

Based in Mwanza, Tanzania

ANCEVY (PVT) LIMITED is headquartered in Mwanza, Tanzania's second-largest city and a major commercial hub situated on the shores of Lake Victoria. Mwanza serves as an important economic center for the surrounding regions, making it an ideal location for serving clients throughout northwestern Tanzania and beyond.

From our strategic location in Nyamhongolo, we serve clients across Mwanza region, Ilemela district, and surrounding areas.
