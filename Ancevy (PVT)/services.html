Our Electrical Services

ANCEVY (PVT) LIMITED provides comprehensive electrical solutions for residential, commercial, and industrial clients across Tanzania. Our Class 4 CRB registration ensures compliance with national standards, and our experienced team delivers quality workmanship on every project.

Residential Electrical

Complete electrical solutions for homes and residential developments, from new construction to renovation projects. We understand that your home's electrical system is critical to your daily life, and we ensure it is safe, efficient, and built to last.

What We Offer:

  • Complete house wiring and rewiring for new construction and renovations
  • Lighting system design and installation including indoor and outdoor lighting
  • Electrical panel upgrades to meet modern power demands
  • Ceiling fan and fixture installation with proper load calculations
  • Electrical safety inspections and compliance verification
  • Outlet and switch installation and replacement
  • Surge protection installation to safeguard your appliances
  • Emergency electrical repairs and troubleshooting services

Commercial Electrical

Professional electrical services for office buildings, retail spaces, hotels, and commercial facilities. We understand that commercial operations require reliable electrical systems, and we deliver solutions that minimize downtime and maximize efficiency.

What We Offer:

  • Office building electrical systems design and installation
  • Retail space lighting and power distribution optimization
  • Emergency lighting systems with battery backup installation
  • Data cabling and network infrastructure for modern offices
  • Energy-efficient LED lighting upgrades to reduce operational costs
  • HVAC electrical connections and control systems
  • Fire alarm and security system electrical integration
  • Electrical load analysis and capacity planning for growing businesses

Industrial Electrical

Heavy-duty electrical infrastructure for factories, manufacturing plants, and industrial operations. Industrial environments demand robust electrical systems that can handle high loads, harsh conditions, and continuous operation requirements.

What We Offer:

  • Power distribution systems design and installation for industrial facilities
  • Motor control centers and variable frequency drive installations
  • Industrial lighting solutions including high-bay and task lighting
  • Machine wiring and installation for production equipment
  • Transformer installation, maintenance, and testing services
  • Busbar systems for high-current industrial applications
  • Safety interlocks and emergency stop system integration
  • Industrial automation and control system electrical infrastructure

Electrical Maintenance

Preventive maintenance and troubleshooting services to ensure continuous and safe electrical operations. Regular maintenance extends equipment life, prevents failures, and ensures safety compliance for your electrical systems.

What We Offer:

  • Preventive maintenance programs tailored to your facility needs
  • Emergency electrical repairs with rapid response times
  • System troubleshooting and fault diagnosis using modern testing equipment
  • Equipment testing, calibration, and performance verification
  • Load balancing and optimization to improve system efficiency
  • Thermal imaging inspections to detect potential issues before failure
  • Cable and connection integrity testing and replacement
  • Maintenance documentation and compliance reporting

Power Distribution

Design and installation of power distribution networks for buildings and facilities of all sizes. Proper power distribution is critical for safety, efficiency, and reliability of electrical systems.

What We Offer:

  • Transformer installation including step-up and step-down transformers
  • Switchgear setup and configuration for various voltage levels
  • Distribution board installation and load management
  • Cable routing, sizing, and termination according to load requirements
  • Load calculation and system design for optimal performance
  • Grounding and earthing system installation for safety
  • Power factor correction and harmonic filtering
  • Backup power system integration including generators and UPS systems

Electrical Inspection

Comprehensive inspection and certification services to ensure compliance with safety standards and regulations. Regular inspections are essential for maintaining safe electrical systems and meeting regulatory requirements.

What We Offer:

  • Pre-purchase electrical inspections for property buyers
  • Safety compliance audits for commercial and industrial facilities
  • CRB compliance verification and documentation support
  • Electrical system certification for new installations
  • Fault finding and detailed reporting with recommendations
  • Periodic inspection scheduling and reminder services
  • Insurance inspection documentation and certification
  • Code compliance verification for building permits and approvals

Our Process

How we deliver quality electrical services from initial consultation to project completion.

1. Consultation

We begin with a thorough consultation to understand your project requirements, timeline, and budget. Our team assesses your needs and provides expert recommendations tailored to your specific situation.

2. Planning & Design

We develop detailed plans and designs for your electrical system, ensuring compliance with all regulations and optimal performance. This includes load calculations, equipment selection, and project scheduling.

3. Installation

Our experienced team executes the installation with precision and attention to detail. We follow safety protocols, use quality materials, and maintain clean work sites throughout the process.

4. Testing & Inspection

Every installation undergoes rigorous testing and inspection to ensure it meets all safety standards and performance requirements. We verify proper operation before project handover.

5. Documentation

We provide comprehensive documentation including test results, compliance certificates, operation manuals, and maintenance schedules to support your long-term system management.

6. Ongoing Support

Our relationship continues after project completion with maintenance support, warranty services, and technical assistance whenever you need it. We stand behind our work.
